Privacy preserving location data publishing: a machine learning approach

Role: Team Leader — ATRI-Hyd (Oct 2022 - Nov 2022)
  • The process of data publication can be highly risky as it may disclose individuals’ sensitive information.
  • A widely accepted privacy for the publication of datasets is k-anonymity.
  • The anonymization scheme is based on achieving k-anonymity by grouping similar trajectories and removing highly dissimilar ones.
  • The anonymisation used is known as MLA (Machine Learning based Anonymisation).
  • Methods used in the preserving include k-means algorithm and generalisation algorithm.
  • The data is unsupervised, so k-means algorithm is used.
  • The dataset used is the taxi trajectory (TDRIVE) dataset. The ID for the data drive is longitude-latitude data location.

Efficient Retrieval over documents encrypted by attributes in Cloud Computing

Role: Team Leader — ATRI-Hyd (Dec 2022 - Jun 2023)
  • Secure document storage and retrieval is one of the hottest research directions in cloud computing.
  • Searchable encryption schemes have been proposed and are under work; few support efficient retrieval over documents encrypted based on their names.
  • A hierarchical attribute-based encryption scheme is first designed for a document collection.
  • A set of documents can be encrypted together if they share an integrated access structure.
  • With ciphertext-policy attribute-based encryption (CP-ABE) schemes, both the ciphertext storage space and time costs of encryption/decryption are saved.
